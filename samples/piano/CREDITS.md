# Piano samples

49 natural-note piano recordings (C1–B7), used to synthesize every note by
pitch-shifting to the nearest sample (at most 1 semitone away).

**Source:** FluidR3_GM soundfont, rendered to MP3 by the
[midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts) project
(`acoustic_grand_piano-mp3`).
